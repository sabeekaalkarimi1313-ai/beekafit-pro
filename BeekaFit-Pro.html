Jogging tips

  • Start with run–walkJog 1 min, walk 2 min, repeat 6–8 times. Add jogging time each week.
  • Talk-test paceYou should be able to speak in short sentences. Gasping means slow down.
  • Beat the heatIn Taiwan, jog before 8 am or after sunset, and drink water before you go.
  • Good routesRiverside parks and school tracks are flat, lit and away from scooters.

When to eat around a workout

  • 1–2 hours beforeSomething light with carbs: a banana, oats, toast, or a roasted sweet potato from 7-Eleven.
  • Within 2 hours afterProtein plus carbs: 2 tea eggs and a sweet potato, chicken with rice, or daal with roti.
  • Skip right before trainingHeavy oily meals like fried chicken or biryani. They sit in your stomach and cause cramps.

Ordering drinks in Taiwan

  • 無糖 wú tángNo sugar. The best choice for tea.
  • 微糖 wéi tángLight sugar, roughly 30%. A good middle step if you are cutting down.
  • 去冰 / 少冰No ice / less ice. Does not change sugar, so still pick a low sugar level.
